Privacy Policy

Effective: 20 October 2026

In short: your videos, captions and edits stay on your iPhone. To create captions, TITR sends only the sound of the video you choose, through TITR’s server, to ElevenLabs, an AI speech-recognition service. It asks you first, and you can withdraw that consent in the app. TITR’s server keeps no audio and no text, only the records listed in section 3.2. It deletes transcription and usage records after 13 months; section 7 says how long each record is kept. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

1.Who we are

TITR is an iPhone app that turns the speech in your videos into captions you can edit. It is made by Rati Liklikadze, an individual developer in Georgia, who decides how your data is used (the “controller”).

Contact for anything in this policy: ratiliklikadze@gmail.com.

TITR has no accounts and never uploads your videos, captions or edits. Deleting a project deletes its files. Deleting the app deletes everything except the two Keychain items: iOS may keep those after the app is deleted, so that a reinstall keeps the same plan. They contain nothing about you.

TITR opens videos through the system photo picker, so it sees only the video you pick, not your library. It asks for permission to add to Photos only when you save an export. It does not use the microphone, the camera, your location or your contacts.

3.What leaves your iPhone, and why

3.1 The sound of a video, to create captions

When you tap Create captions, TITR extracts the sound of that one video (mono, compressed audio) and sends it, through TITR’s server, to ElevenLabs, an AI speech-recognition service. ElevenLabs returns the words and their timings, and TITR shows them as captions.

What is sent
The audio track of the video you chose, its length and the spoken language you picked. Never the picture
When
Only after you agree on the “Before your first captions” screen, and each time you create captions or try again
Who receives it
TITR’s server (Cloudflare) passes it on to ElevenLabs. ElevenLabs receives it from TITR’s own account, with no identifier of you or your iPhone
Kept by TITR?
No. TITR’s server holds the audio only while the request runs, and it stores neither the audio nor the text that comes back
At ElevenLabs
ElevenLabs processes the audio to produce the transcript, under ElevenLabs’ terms and its privacy policy (https://elevenlabs.io/privacy-policy). TITR does not store the audio

If you don’t agree, nothing is sent, but TITR cannot create captions.

3.2 Records on TITR’s server

To run your plan’s minutes fairly and to stop abuse, TITR’s server keeps:

  • For each install: a random install identifier, a fingerprint (SHA-256) of its session token (never the token itself), the random app user identifier TITR created on your iPhone, and when the install started.
  • For each transcription: a random job identifier, its status, the seconds reserved and charged, how often it was retried, the provider and model used, what it cost TITR, the outcome code, how long it took, and the times.
  • Counters: seconds used per month, and what each install cost TITR per day (used to cap spending).
  • If you subscribe: what RevenueCat reports about the subscription: the product, the plan, the current period’s start and end, whether it is active, will renew or has a billing issue, the store, whether it was a test (sandbox) purchase, the App Store’s original transaction identifier, and the identifier and time of the last update.
  • Subscription updates received: the update’s identifier, type and time.

These records contain no audio, no text, no video, no name, no email address, no Apple Account details and no payment details.

The app user identifier is a random code that TITR creates once on your iPhone. It ties a subscription to your minutes. It is not your Apple Account and says nothing about who you are.

3.3 Device check

So that reinstalling the app does not reset the free minutes, TITR uses Apple’s DeviceCheck. The app sends a one-time device token to TITR’s server, which asks Apple to check it and to read or set two yes/no values that Apple keeps for TITR on that iPhone (for example, “free minutes already started this month”). TITR does not store the token, and Apple does not tell TITR who you are or which iPhone it is.

3.4 Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple. TITR never sees your payment details, your name or your Apple Account.

TITR uses RevenueCat to check subscriptions. The app sends RevenueCat the app user identifier and your App Store purchase records, along with the basic technical data that comes with each request (such as the app and iOS version and your IP address). RevenueCat then tells TITR’s server which plan is active.

RevenueCat also gives TITR statistics about its subscriptions, such as how many people subscribe, renew or cancel. They are counted under the app user identifier. TITR uses them to see how its plans sell, never for advertising.

3.5 Server logs and network

Like any internet service, TITR’s server and its host, Cloudflare, process your IP address to deliver each request. TITR’s database does not store it. TITR’s server writes short logs (install and job identifiers, outcome codes, timings), which never contain audio, text, tokens or keys. Cloudflare keeps these logs for up to 7 days.

4.What TITR does not do

  • No advertising and no ad profiles.
  • No tracking: no data is linked with other companies’ data for advertising, and nothing is shared with data brokers.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting tools in the app, apart from RevenueCat’s subscription statistics (section 3.4).
  • No selling or renting of data.
  • No accounts, no sign-in and no email collection in the app.

8.Your choices and rights

  • Withdraw consent to send audio: in TITR, Settings › Privacy › Withdraw consent to send audio. Nothing more is sent until you agree again, and TITR asks before the next upload. Audio already processed is not affected.
  • Delete my data: in TITR, Settings › Privacy › Delete my data deletes the install record, its transcription and usage records, its free-minute counter and its spending counter from TITR’s server at once. It does not delete:
    • the subscription status and the minutes a subscription has used, so that deleting cannot refill a paid month. The minutes used are deleted 13 months after their last update; the subscription status stays until you ask us to delete it;
    • the free minutes this iPhone already used this month (DeviceCheck);
    • anything on your iPhone. Your projects stay until you delete them.
  • Access, correction, deletion, objection, a copy of your data: write to ratiliklikadze@gmail.com. TITR has no accounts, so to find a subscription’s records we may ask for the order ID from Apple’s purchase receipt email.
  • Complaints: you can complain to a data protection authority: in Georgia, the Personal Data Protection Service; in the EU, the authority where you live.

9.Children

TITR is not made for children and does not knowingly collect data from them. If you are under 16, ask a parent or guardian before you agree to send audio. If you think a child has used TITR without a parent’s or guardian’s agreement, you can delete the records on TITR’s server at once in TITR, Settings › Privacy › Delete my data. You can also write to ratiliklikadze@gmail.com.

10.Security

All traffic between the app and TITR’s server is encrypted (HTTPS). The server stores session tokens only as fingerprints, and the keys for ElevenLabs and the other services stay on the server, never in the app. On your iPhone, the token and the app user identifier are kept in the Keychain.

11.Changes to this policy

When the data flows change, we update this page and its date. If a change affects what leaves your iPhone, TITR tells you in the app and asks for your consent again before it sends any audio.

3.3 მოწყობილობის შემოწმება

იმისთვის, რომ აპის ხელახლა დაყენებით უფასო წუთები თავიდან არ ჩაირიცხოს, TITR იყენებს Apple-ის DeviceCheck-ს. აპი TITR-ის სერვერს უგზავნის მოწყობილობის ერთჯერად ტოკენს, სერვერი კი Apple-ს სთხოვს, შეამოწმოს ის და წაიკითხოს ან ჩაწეროს ორი „კი/არა“ მნიშვნელობა, რომლებსაც Apple ამ iPhone-ზე TITR-ისთვის ინახავს (მაგალითად, „ამ თვის უფასო წუთები უკვე დაწყებულია“). TITR ტოკენს არ ინახავს, Apple კი TITR-ს არ ეუბნება, ვინ ხართ ან რომელი iPhone არის.

3.4 შესყიდვები

გამოწერებს Apple ყიდის და ანგარიშსწორებასაც Apple აწარმოებს. TITR ვერასდროს ხედავს თქვენს გადახდის მონაცემებს, სახელს ან Apple Account-ს.

გამოწერების შესამოწმებლად TITR იყენებს RevenueCat-ს. აპი RevenueCat-ს უგზავნის მომხმარებლის იდენტიფიკატორსა და თქვენი შესყიდვების ჩანაწერებს App Store-დან, ასევე ძირითად ტექნიკურ მონაცემებს, რომლებიც ყოველ მოთხოვნას ახლავს (მაგალითად, აპისა და iOS-ის ვერსიასა და თქვენს IP მისამართს). შემდეგ RevenueCat TITR-ის სერვერს აცნობებს, რომელი გეგმაა აქტიური.

RevenueCat ასევე გვაწვდის TITR-ის გამოწერების სტატისტიკას, მაგალითად, ახალი გამოწერების, განახლებებისა და გაუქმებების რაოდენობას. ეს მონაცემები მომხმარებლის იდენტიფიკატორით აღირიცხება. TITR მათ იყენებს იმის გასაგებად, თუ როგორ იყიდება მისი გეგმები, და არასდროს იყენებს რეკლამისთვის.
