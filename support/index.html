TITR support

TITR puts captions on your videos, word for word. It is made for Georgian first, and it works for English too.

Contact

Email ratiliklikadze@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, your iOS version and TITR’s version (at the bottom of TITR’s Settings).

TITR is made by Rati Liklikadze, an individual developer in Georgia.

Creating captions

How do I start?

Tap +, pick a video, choose the spoken language (Georgian or English) and tap Create captions. The first time, TITR asks before it sends the video’s sound.

Which languages does TITR understand?

Georgian and English speech. Pick the one spoken in the video before you tap Create captions; the wrong choice gives wrong words.

Do I need the internet?

Only to create captions. Editing and exporting work offline.

It says “This video is longer than your plan allows.”

Each plan has a longest video it can caption, and the Free plan’s is the shortest. The plans screen (Settings › See plans) shows each paid plan’s. Trim the video in Photos, or choose a plan with longer videos.

Creating captions failed.

Tap Try again. When creating captions fails, no minutes are used.

Fixing captions

A word is wrong.

Tap it and type the right word. Words TITR is unsure about are marked; Review uncertain words takes you through them one by one.

The same word is wrong everywhere.

Use Find and replace to fix it in the whole video at once.

A caption shows too early or too late.

Select it on the caption track under the video and drag its edges.

Undo works for every change.

Exporting

Which quality should I pick?

Original quality keeps your video’s resolution and frame rate, and HDR stays HDR. Most compatible plays everywhere; HDR becomes standard range.

Save to Photos doesn’t work.

TITR needs permission to add to Photos. Allow it in the iPhone’s Settings › Apps › TITR › Photos, or use Share instead.

Does exporting use minutes?

No. Exports are always free and unlimited, with no watermark.

Long videos keep exporting if you leave TITR. Closing TITR from the app switcher stops the export.

Plans and purchases

What do the plans include?

TITR’s Settings shows your plan’s minutes for the month and how many are left. See plans shows each paid plan’s minutes per month and longest video. The Free plan needs no purchase. Minutes count the length of the sound that is captioned. Every plan’s minutes renew each month, yearly plans included.

I bought a plan, but the minutes didn’t change.

Wait a moment, then tap Restore purchases in TITR’s Settings. If they still haven’t changed, email us with the order ID from Apple’s receipt email.

I have a new iPhone.

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open TITR’s Settings and tap Restore purchases.

How do I cancel?

In the iPhone’s Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions › TITR. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Language

How do I switch TITR to English (or back to Georgian)?

In TITR’s Settings, tap App language. It opens the iPhone’s Settings › Apps › TITR, where you choose the language. If that page has no Language row (iOS can hide it when the iPhone has only one language), add a second language in the iPhone’s Settings › General › Language & Region; the row then appears. The spoken language for new videos is a separate setting in TITR’s Settings.

Privacy

Your videos, captions and edits stay on your iPhone. To create captions, TITR sends only the sound of the video, through TITR’s server, to ElevenLabs, an AI speech-recognition service. TITR’s server keeps no audio and no text.

  • Withdraw consent: TITR › Settings › Privacy › Withdraw consent to send audio. TITR asks again before it sends any audio.
  • Delete your data on TITR’s server: TITR › Settings › Privacy › Delete my data.
